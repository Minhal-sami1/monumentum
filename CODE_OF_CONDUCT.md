# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be direct, be specific, be kind. Critique the work, not the person. Show
your evidence. Assume good faith, and say so when you cannot.

## Expected

- Respect people regardless of background, identity, or experience.
- Give feedback that names the problem and, where you can, the fix.
- Accept correction without defensiveness, and correct yourself in public
  when you were wrong. This project records its own mistakes in
  `docs/DECISIONS.md`; hold yourself to the same standard.

## Not accepted

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualised content,
publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer named in
`pyproject.toml`. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may
warn, remove content, or ban, in proportion to the harm.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and when representing the project in
public.
